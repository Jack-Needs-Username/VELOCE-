Veloce
My Garage Model Library Digest

Research method

How Veloce teaches car ownership.

Veloce separates profile learning, model facts, editorial guidance, and market context so users know what kind of information they are reading.

Verified facts

Production years, engine layout, drivetrain, body style, and historical context are tied to citations.

Editorial guidance

Ownership notes explain the likely owner fit, maintenance posture, and driving use case as editorial context.

Market context

Marketplace links lead to Classic.com, Hemmings, Bring a Trailer, and ClassicCars.com for current listings and comps.

Source refresh

How Digest stays current.

Digest reads from approved RSS/source adapters and a static cache in data/digest-cache.json. Users can refresh sources in-browser for the current session; the deployed GitHub Pages cache is refreshed by the scheduled workflow in .github/workflows/refresh-real-data-cache.yml.

Vehicle matching

New cars automatically enter Digest matching.

Digest does not store fixed car/article pairings. It compares real article headlines, excerpts, tags, and source text against the canonical vehicle library, including make, model, display name, aliases, categories, tags, related models, and the user's saved Garage.

Why no Firebase yet

Local storage is enough for this build.

The current prototype stores favorites and garage saves in the browser. Firebase should come when the app needs real cross-device accounts, shared garages, or authenticated event recommendations.

Veloce is designed to grow into account-based profiles later.
